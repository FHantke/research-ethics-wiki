# Research Ethics Wiki

## Overview

This wiki is a comprehensive collection of information regarding research ethics in computer security and privacy research. It aims to provide researchers, practitioners, and students with a resource for understanding and navigating the ethical considerations inherent in conducting security and privacy research.

## What is Research Ethics?

Research ethics encompasses the moral principles and professional standards that guide the conduct of research. In the context of computer security and privacy research, ethical considerations are particularly important due to:

1. The potential impact on human subjects and their privacy
2. The dual-use nature of security research findings
3. The legal and regulatory landscape surrounding security research
4. The responsibility to minimize harm while advancing knowledge

## Key Ethical Principles

Research ethics in computer security and privacy is guided by several fundamental principles:

### Respect for Persons
Respecting the autonomy, dignity, and rights of individuals who may be affected by research activities. This includes obtaining informed consent when appropriate and protecting the privacy of research subjects.

### Beneficence
Maximizing benefits and minimizing potential harms. Researchers should carefully consider the potential positive and negative impacts of their work on individuals, organizations, and society.

### Justice
Ensuring that the benefits and burdens of research are distributed fairly. Research should not exploit vulnerable populations or unfairly advantage certain groups.

### Responsible Disclosure
When security vulnerabilities are discovered, researchers have a responsibility to disclose them in a manner that allows for remediation while minimizing potential harm.

## Why This Wiki?

The landscape of research ethics in computer security and privacy is complex and constantly evolving. This wiki serves to:

- Provide guidance on ethical frameworks and principles
- Document best practices for conducting ethical research
- Offer resources on institutional review processes (IRB/Ethics Committees)
- Address specific ethical challenges in security and privacy research
- Share case studies and lessons learned from the research community
- Keep the community updated on evolving ethical standards and regulations

## Topics Covered

This wiki covers a range of topics including:

- **Ethical Frameworks**: Different approaches to evaluating the ethics of security and privacy research
- **IRB and Ethics Review**: Understanding and navigating institutional review processes
- **Informed Consent**: When and how to obtain consent in security/privacy research
- **Vulnerability Research**: Ethical considerations in discovering and disclosing security vulnerabilities
- **Data Collection and Privacy**: Ethical handling of personal and sensitive data
- **Human Subjects Research**: Special considerations when research involves human participants
- **Legal Considerations**: Understanding the legal landscape and its intersection with ethics
- **Publication Ethics**: Ethical considerations in publishing security research
