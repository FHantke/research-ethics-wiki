                                        User Studies#

                                        From S&P: Submissions that describe experiments that could be viewed as involving human subjects, that analyze data derived from human subjects (even anonymized data), or that otherwise may put humans at risk should:

                                        Disclose whether the research received an approval or waiver from each of the authors’ institutional ethics review boards (e.g., IRB) if applicable. Discuss steps taken to ensure that participants and others who might have been affected by an experiment were treated ethically and with respect. If a submission deals with any kind of personal identifiable information (PII) or other kinds of sensitive data, the version of the paper submitted for review must discuss in detail the steps the authors have taken to mitigate harms to the persons identified. If a paper raises significant ethical and/or legal concerns, it will be checked by the REC and it might be rejected based on these concerns. The PC chairs will be happy to consult with authors about how this policy applies to their submissions.
